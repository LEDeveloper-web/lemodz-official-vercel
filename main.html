About LEMODZ

Who we are

LEMODZ is the official brand for high-quality Minecraft Bedrock Edition add-ons created by Lanceefi2011 (Owner) with development support from Grok AI.

Our goal is to deliver fun, stable, and feature-rich mods — from admin tools and economy systems to creative weather events — all built for Bedrock with Beta APIs.

Requirements

  • Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21+
  • Experiments → Beta APIs = ON
  • Cheats enabled for admin commands
  • Tag yourself: /tag @s add admin
  • v1.0.1+: use /le:help or bare aliases like /smite
